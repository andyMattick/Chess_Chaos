## Party matches with the new secret-mission rule (8 games per mode, ★800 + buffs vs ★1200 + handicaps)

- **Underdog won 56 of 208 (27%), down from 98 of 208 (47%) before the rule change.** Missions were the underdog's main way to win; now that a shown mission can be undone (and the bots try to undo it), fewer games end that way.
- Missions shown 58 times: undone 29, won 25, the game ended another way 4.
- No mission is "never undone" or "always undone". Pawn pile-up (7 kept, 4 undone) and Runaway pawn (7 kept, 9 undone) are the most often kept; Skewer (2 kept, 5 undone) and Knight fortress (2 kept, 5 undone) the least.
- Pawn wars: the favourite won 8 of 8 (avg 7 moves): the race to promote leaves no room for buffs or missions.
