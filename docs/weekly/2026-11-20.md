The app was fine. Fixed in the script (pull request #1, merged). After the fix all 16 steps pass, no page errors, no sideways scroll.

Looking at the screenshots as a 9-year-old:

1. **The Bot Path intro is hard to read:** "After its favourite opening, each bot plays with the game's own chess engine at its own strength." "Opening" and "engine" need explaining, or the line could be cut to "Beat a bot to meet the next one!"
2. **Puzzles opens on an error when Chess.com does not answer** ("Chess.com is not answering right now"). A child sees a sleepy puzzle piece first. Leading with the built-in warm-ups (the 49 engine-checked puzzles) would avoid that.
3. **Puzzles, Game modes and Level-Up sit below three daily-mission cards** on a phone; a new player has to scroll past "0 / 3" bars to find them. The in-game pieces are also small on a phone and look alike; "Plain pieces" helps but is easy to miss.
